⚓ Anchor
Camera access

Anchor can notice when you look away from your screen for a few seconds. It uses your camera to check whether a face is visible and which way it is turned.

Video is processed on your device and is never stored or uploaded. Only simple events, like how long you looked away, are kept for your session summary.

When Chrome asks, choose Allow (not “Allow this time”) so Anchor can keep using the camera during your session.
